Technical Philosophy

How I Think About AI Systems

Three principles that guide every architecture decision I make.

01
Production first, not demo first
A model that works in a notebook is not a product. I design for latency, throughput, drift, failure modes, and audit trails from day one. Compliance and observability are architecture concerns, not afterthoughts.
02
Tradeoffs over best practices
Best practice says "use a vector DB." The actual question is: what retrieval latency can your SLA tolerate, and what does a hallucination cost your business? Every architectural choice has a tradeoff. I name the tradeoff before I name the tool.
03
Business impact is the only real metric
AUC going from 0.88 to 0.91 is not a result. A 12–18% conversion improvement across millions of banking customers is a result. I optimize for the number that appears in a business review, not just the number in a model card.
